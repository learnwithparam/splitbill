## Problem

After a trip, people have to eyeball the balances list and figure out who
should pay whom to even things out. It's slow and error-prone with more
than 2-3 people.

## Desired outcome

Settling up should be easier. Not sure exactly what that looks like yet -
maybe a clearer summary, maybe something else.
